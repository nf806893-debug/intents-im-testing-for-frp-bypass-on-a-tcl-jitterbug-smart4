About this tester

These links request standard Android settings activities. Chrome, Android, or the device manufacturer may block or reject them. A settings screen may also be restricted during device setup.

Smart Lock and other privileged setup activities are deliberately omitted because their availability varies by device and firmware. This page does not remove Factory Reset Protection.
